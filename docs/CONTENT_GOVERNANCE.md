# Content Governance

## Purpose

ORIGINA is a science-led institution. Public copy is therefore governed like institutional communication, not ordinary promotional copy.

The content registry in `config/origina_content.php` is the frontend source for routes using the shared institutional renderer. Dedicated pages follow the same rules.

## Authority order

For public content:

1. explicit current instruction from the ORIGINA owner;
2. approved institutional/scientific source material;
3. approved content already present in `origina-next`;
4. conservative wording derived from documented project context.

Never invent missing proof.

## Scientific claims

Do not independently introduce or strengthen:

- clinical efficacy;
- “clinically proven” language;
- disease-treatment claims;
- trial outcomes;
- regulatory approval;
- certifications;
- patent status;
- manufacturing certifications;
- superiority claims;
- safety guarantees.

The strength of a claim must correspond to the strength of the available evidence.

## Evidence status

Use explicit distinctions such as:

- hypothesis;
- in-vitro;
- ex-vivo;
- formulation testing;
- instrumental/biophysical testing;
- pilot human evaluation;
- controlled clinical study;
- randomized controlled trial;
- systematic evidence.

Do not compress these into a generic “scientifically proven” label.

## Intellectual property

Keep these terms distinct:

- trademarked;
- proprietary;
- trade secret / know-how;
- patent application;
- granted patent.

The ™ symbol does not establish patent status.

## Regulatory language

Regulatory classification precedes claims.

For Tanzania, public wording must distinguish the applicable roles of TBS and TMDA and must not state “approved” without underlying documentation.

Regulatory information should be re-verified before a production publication that materially relies on current law, classification or authority scope.

## Future institutions and divisions

Planned/future directions such as Academy, Ventures, Research Institute and Foundation must be visibly identified as planned, future or open possibility until the organization confirms that they are operating.

Do not convert roadmap language into present-tense operating claims.

## Founder and people

Founder storytelling should reinforce scientific leadership and institution-building rather than celebrity positioning.

Credentials must match approved source material exactly.

## Privacy and health information

General website contact is for institutional enquiries.

Do not invite users to submit:

- clinical-trial participant data;
- medical records;
- detailed health histories;
- other sensitive health information

through a general contact channel.

Dedicated research/privacy/consent workflows belong to a later approved backend/research architecture.

## Editorial quality

Every public page should answer:

- What institutional question does this page resolve?
- What is operating now?
- What is research/development?
- What is future?
- What evidence supports the language?
- What should the reader do next?

Prefer precise, calm language over hype.

## Review triggers

A content review is mandatory when a change affects:

- scientific or clinical claims;
- product claims;
- regulatory status;
- patents/IP status;
- founder credentials;
- operating status of a future entity;
- privacy/data handling;
- pricing or availability once commerce exists.
